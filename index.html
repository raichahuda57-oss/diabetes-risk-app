DiaBD Risk Screening

A research prototype estimating diabetes risk from routine clinical measurements, built on the DiaBD dataset (Bangladesh).

Not a diagnosis. This is a university sessional-project prototype trained on a public research dataset. Results are statistical estimates, not medical advice — always consult a qualified doctor for actual testing and diagnosis.

Why this result? (feature contribution)

Positive values push the estimate toward higher risk; negative values push it toward lower risk. This reflects statistical association learned by the model, not medical causation.

Model: Logistic Regression · 9 consensus-selected features · trained on SMOTENC-balanced data · tuned decision threshold (0.407) · held-out test ROC-AUC 0.82, recall 0.68. Predictions are computed live by the local model server (api.py) — nothing leaves your computer.
